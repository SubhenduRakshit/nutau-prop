# v0.9 JHEP manuscript

`main.tex` is the versioned research draft and `main.pdf` is its compiled output.
The `jheppub.sty` and `JHEP.bst` files are unmodified publisher files from
https://jhep.sissa.it/jhep/help/JHEP_TeXclass.jsp, downloaded 2026-10-27.
Build with `latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex`.

This draft reports a public-data screening result. The exact IceCube EHE
likelihood requires unreleased reconstructed-bin response and background files.
