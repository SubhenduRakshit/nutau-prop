User question: can leptoquarks generate a similar resonance and serve the original UHE goal?

**Answer:** yes to the resonance mechanism; potentially to a significant viable UHE deviation, but no all-constraints-surviving point is certified here. Scalar leptoquarks deserve parallel consideration because their neutrino-quark vertices are renormalizable. This is an advantage over the dimension-five leptogluon transition, whose large coefficient still requires UV matching.

This follow-up used chat-side literature retrieval and a handful of remote Wolfram PDF evaluations. No local jobs, production-code changes, or workflows were launched. All figures below are provisional analytic narrow-width screens.

## 1. Analytic resonance and normalization

For a scalar leptoquark with one physical Yukawa vertex lambda coupling a neutrino to a quark or antiquark, the partonic resonance is at
\[
 \hat s=2m_N E_\nu x=M^2,\qquad
 x_*=\frac{M^2}{2m_NE_\nu}.
\]
This is a partonic pole; integration over the broad quark distribution does not give a monochromatic neutrino-energy line.

With massless external fermions and a fixed colour of the scalar,
\[
 \Gamma_{\nu q}=\frac{|\lambda|^2M}{16\pi}.
\]
The scalar decay spin sum is |lambda|² M². Averaging the incoming quark spin divides by two; do not average the single physical neutrino helicity. The colour sum cancels the initial-quark colour average. The one-particle phase space then yields
\[
 \hat\sigma(\nu q_{\rm res}\to S\to X)
 =\frac{\pi|\lambda|^2}{2}
   \delta(\hat s-M^2)\,B_X ,
\]
\[
 \boxed{\sigma_{\rm res}(\nu N\to S\to X)=
 \frac{\pi|\lambda|^2}{2s}\,q_{\rm res}(x_*,M^2)B_X
 =\frac{\pi|\lambda|^2}{2M^2}
 [xq_{\rm res}(x,M^2)]_{x=x_*}B_X .}
\]
Dimensions are GeV^-2. A convention averaging an unphysical second neutrino helicity would instead introduce an erroneous factor 1/2 for this beam definition.

For the equal neutrino/antineutrino average used in our comparison, replace xq_res by x(q+qbar)/2. S1 neutrinos resonate on down-type quarks; R2 neutrinos on up-type antiquarks; tilde-R2 neutrinos on down-type antiquarks. Their charge conjugates interchange quarks and antiquarks. This distinction matters for a non-equal beam or valence flavours.

Summing all physical decay channels sets B_X=1 for inclusive production. A specific CC-like or NC-like final state requires its own branching fraction. The scalar decay is isotropic in its rest frame; at the massless pole its final-state y distribution is flat, with mean y=1/2. Consequently the NC branch has an active daughter and appreciable energy redistribution; it is not a sterile sink.

These normalizations agree with the spin/partial-width treatment in [Bai, Xie and Zhou, arXiv:2510.13948v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13948v2), Appendix IV C. Their event forecasts do not establish allowed parameters for the tau-specific benchmarks below.

## 2. Which gauge-invariant models?

| Model | Representation (SU3, SU2, Y) | Illustrative interaction | Neutrino topology and main issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | (anti-3,1,1/3) | lambda bar(Q_2^c) i tau_2 L_tau S1 + h.c. | nu_tau s resonance; both active NC and tau-producing CC decays. CKM-linked charged currents constrain it. |
| R2 | (3,2,7/6) | lambda bar(c_R) R2^T i tau_2 L_tau + h.c. | nu_tau anti-c resonance; NC-only resonant component for this single coupling. The partner couples to c tau. |
| tilde-R2 | (3,2,1/6) | lambda bar(s_R) tilde-R2^T i tau_2 L_tau + h.c. | nu_tau anti-s resonance; active NC decay. The partner couples to s tau. |

S1 is sometimes defined as the conjugate field, changing the tabulated signs of its representation but not the physics. Impose baryon number to forbid its possible diquark couplings.

The minimal R2 and tilde-R2 interactions involve one right-handed quark species and one lepton doublet. Their two weak components couple separately to the neutrino and charged lepton. An additional R2 Yukawa can open a CC decay but also introduces further flavour/EDM constraints.

**Priority for the cross-section/transport goal:** examine a tau-aligned tilde-R2 with a single strange-quark coupling, alongside the leptogluon. It supplies a renormalizable, gauge-invariant NC resonance without a tree-level charged-current charm-decay amplitude from that single coupling. This structural advantage does not remove the s-tau partner, ditau limits, missing-energy searches or loop constraints.

For a tau-production goal S1 is more direct: with only its left-doublet Yukawa and negligible final masses, CKM unitarity gives approximately equal total branching fractions to nu+s and tau+up-type quarks. Its stronger charged-current flavour relations must be included. A direct muon-track signal is not generated by a tau-only coupling, although tau decays produce secondary muons/neutrinos.

Vector leptoquarks such as U1 can also resonate, but a predictive high-energy analysis requires a gauge completion and its extra states. Introducing a massive vector with arbitrary couplings is not an automatic UV solution.

Quark luminosities and the scalar production factor make these smaller than the earlier leptogluon benchmark. This comparison is not a universal ranking across models.

A deliberately stronger **size-only benchmark**, M=1.5 TeV, lambda=2, gives:

| Channel | Extra pb at 100 PeV | Extra pb at 300 PeV | Extra / SM total |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| strange | 499.102 | 1010.463 | 7.17% / 9.71% |
| charm | 418.133 | 892.028 | 6.01% / 8.57% |

For a single-decay-channel R2 or tilde-R2 component, Gamma/M=lambda²/(16 pi)=0.0796 at lambda=2. The NWA is only a screen at this width; its error cannot be asserted below 8% without a finite-width calculation and PDF-slope check. S1 with only its left Yukawa has two sets of channels, Gamma/M approximately 0.159, making an NWA interpretation less controlled. Its inclusive numbers above must also be divided between CC and NC topology.

At M=2 TeV, lambda=2, the strange-channel results are 182.328/405.303 pb. The couplings required to reach 5% in this NWA at that mass are approximately 2.763 at 100 PeV and 2.266 at 300 PeV. Increasing the coupling also increases width and strengthens collider effects; these are required-coupling estimates, not allowed limits.

No physical claim is made that any stronger benchmark survives the constraints below. In particular, lying above one pair-production mass bound is not sufficient.

## 4. Constraints that decide viability

- [Kirk, Okawa and Wu, arXiv:2307.11152v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.11152v2) reassesses R2 and S1 using collider, flavour and precision constraints, including previously omitted tau-decay information. It finds collider dominance for the studied textures. Its conclusion is neither a proof that all leptoquarks are excluded nor permission to reuse an older large benchmark. Its Appendix A also examines the single-Yukawa R2 limit.
- [ATLAS arXiv:2303.09444](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09444) searches for tau plus charm/light-quark pairs and excludes the stated scalar-LQ benchmark below 1.3 TeV for unit branching fraction to charm+tau. It does not use flavour tagging in the signal region. The charged partner of our doublet must therefore be treated explicitly; arbitrary splitting also encounters electroweak precision and scalar-potential constraints.
- [CMS EXO-22-018 / arXiv:2308.06143](https://cms-results.web.cern.ch/cms-results/public-results/publications/EXO-22-018/index.html) tests resonant tau-quark production with 138 fb^-1 and supplies coupling-versus-mass limits. Its light-quark assumptions must be matched to a strange-only model; no numerical exclusion curve has been transferred in this checkpoint.
- Ditau tails from the charged partner's t-channel exchange, jet+missing-energy pair decays of the neutrino component, and single production must be combined. A light-flavour label does not imply equal u,d,s,c sensitivity: proton PDFs differ substantially.
- A single strange-right-handed texture avoids tree-level b->s transitions unless other flavour couplings are introduced. Radiative flavour effects and electroweak/lepton-universality corrections remain to be checked. Flavour alignment is an assumption, not an all-orders exemption.
- Current electron/muon LQ exclusions, for example [ATLAS arXiv:2507.03650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03650), cannot simply be applied to tau-only couplings; conversely their existence is a reason to retain lepton-initiated production in the audit.
- UHE cross sections alone do not determine IceCube or KM3NeT event counts. Reweighting SM detector effective areas by total sigma is insufficient when the CC/NC branching and daughter-energy distribution change.

Thus the strange-only tilde-R2 is a concrete candidate for the next analytic/constraint check, not a declared survivor. Current evidence does not establish the requested simultaneous laboratory, collider, EFT/perturbativity and neutrino compatibility.

## 5. Approximation and reproduction record

The NWA retains the pole contribution. Full predictions require off-shell and crossed diagrams, SM interference where present, and consistently matched QCD corrections. Do not add a gluon-initiated real-emission channel as an independent enhancement without treating its overlap with the quark PDF and the remaining NLO terms. Resonant pole enhancement is conceptually distinct from extrapolating a contact interaction beyond its validity.

At M=2 TeV the threshold is about 2.13 PeV, and x_* is 0.0213/0.00710 at 100/300 PeV. The kernel reduces the PDF fold analytically to one evaluation; an expensive integration or propagation run is unnecessary to answer this first screening question.
