# Historical output: UHE interpretation superseded by v0.86

These files are preserved unchanged. Their Eq. 4.3 UHE input used twice the
legacy Eq. 4.4 kernel. Exact scattering and decay traces for the specified
distinct-state chiral transition instead require four times the normalized
Eq. 4.4 kernel, itself sixteen times smaller than the inherited one.
Thus the old fixed-coupling Eq. 4.3 UHE rates are overstated by eight.

The width formulas and existing fixed-coupling collider calibrations are not
invalidated by this DIS correction. The interpretation as >=5% UHE points is.
Use `PKR_NORMALIZATION_RESOLUTION_v0.86.md` and `results/v0.86_normalization/`.
