  *Atmospheric and astrophysical neutrinos above 1 TeV interacting in IceCube*,
  Phys. Rev. D 91, 022001 (2015), arXiv:1410.1749.

  contained-event effective areas in true neutrino energy and zenith.


## v0.5 external constraints and future detector scaling

- M. Atzori Corona et al., *Standard Model Tested with Neutrinos*,
  Phys. Rev. Lett. 135, 231803 (2025), arXiv:2504.05272.
  Global-fit tau charge-radius 1σ interval:
  -20 < <r_nu_tau^2>[10^-32 cm^2] < 20.

- IceCube-Gen2 Collaboration public detector description:
  optical volume ~8 km^3, overall sensitivity improvement ~5,
  and 5-10 times as many neutrinos above 30 TeV.

- IceCube-Gen2 Technical Design Report, Volume 1 (2023):
  projected identified tau-neutrino rate above 300 TeV >=1/year,
  compared with ~0.2/year in IceCube.

- G.-Y. Huang, S. Jana, M. Lindner, W. Rodejohann,
  *Probing New Physics at Future Tau Neutrino Telescopes*,
  JCAP 02 (2022) 038, arXiv:2112.09476.
